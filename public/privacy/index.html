Free Together Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Free Together collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our website, applications, and related services.

This is a practical starter policy, not a guarantee of compliance in every jurisdiction. You should have counsel review it before launch, especially if you expect users in California, the EU, the UK, or other regulated markets.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • name and display name;
  • email address;
  • password and authentication credentials handled through Supabase Auth;
  • group names, invite details, and member email addresses you enter;
  • availability and scheduling information you submit.

Information collected automatically

  • basic device, browser, and log information;
  • IP address and approximate network location information;
  • technical event and error data needed to secure and operate the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • create and manage accounts;
  • authenticate users and keep the service secure;
  • create groups, send invites, and store availability data;
  • display overlapping free times and other scheduling results;
  • provide support, troubleshoot bugs, and improve reliability;
  • enforce our Terms and protect the service, users, and infrastructure;
  • comply with legal obligations.

3. We Do Not Sell Personal Data

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not operate a data broker service, and we do not share personal information for third-party advertising sales.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • with other members of a group, as needed for the scheduling features to work;
  • with service providers that help us operate the service, such as Supabase and Vercel;
  • if required by law, court order, subpoena, or legal process;
  • to protect rights, safety, security, or investigate fraud or abuse;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset sale.

5. Service Providers

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and backend infrastructure. We use Vercel for hosting and deployment. Those providers may process information on our behalf in accordance with their own terms and policies.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. We may retain backup copies and security logs for a limited period after deletion requests or account closure.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Free Together is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of certain personal information, or to object to or limit certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the information below.

You can also stop using the service at any time. If supported in the product at the time of your request, you may be able to update or remove certain information directly from your account.

10. International Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States, depending on the infrastructure and providers we use.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version here and revise the effective date. Continued use of the service after the updated policy becomes effective means you accept the updated policy.

For privacy requests or questions, contact: privacy@freetogether.app
